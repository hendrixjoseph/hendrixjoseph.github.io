title: It's All Metaphors
tags: [culture]
---

A computer thinks. Evolution selects. An invisible hand guides the market.

The English language - and I suspect language in general - just isn't capable of describing something new with existing words. So people come up metaphors the describe the thing.

The alternative would be to use similes, but that just gets awkward. It's like a computer thinks. Animals evolve as if they were being selected. Prices go up and down as much as a puppeteer's hand.

The problem lies in that once people start using the metaphors, people soon forget that these were metaphors to begin with. Granted, it's often not the same people, but it's a problem nevertheless.

People begin to think that computers really think, and that they might even be conscious. Or they think that evolution really chose that adaptation, rather than the other failed adaptations went away. Or that there really is some shadow behind why prices are what they are.

English has it's shortcomings. But if we couldn't use these metaphors, we couldn't describe these new things. But we mustn't forget that's what they are, pure and simple - metaphors.
